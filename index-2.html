About the Company

A team of two, held to the standards of a much bigger crew

AFAB Constructions is a Lahore-based construction company led by Directors Abdullah Imtiaz and Afaq Hussain. The company takes on residential construction and renovation work directly, so every project runs through hands that are personally accountable for it — from the first site visit to the final coat of paint.

Direct oversight

Both directors stay involved on active sites rather than handing work off to layers of subcontractors.

Full-scope capability

Structural work, interiors, flooring and demolition are handled under one roof, so nothing falls between contractors.

Lahore-focused

Local knowledge of materials, labour and site conditions across Lahore shapes every estimate and timeline.
